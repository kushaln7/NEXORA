Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you build a microsite?

Our rapid deployment pipeline delivers production-ready microsites in 72 hours for standard campaigns. Complex interactive experiences typically take 1-2 weeks.

Can the microsite connect to my CRM?

Yes. We pre-configure integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, and other platforms so leads flow directly into your pipeline.

What happens after the campaign ends?

We archive the microsite, export all lead data, and provide a comprehensive campaign performance report. The site can be reactivated for future campaigns.
