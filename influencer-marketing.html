Frequently Asked Questions

How do you find the right influencers?

We use data platforms to analyze creator audiences by demographics, interests, engagement rate, and audience authenticity. We never select creators based on follower count alone.

What platforms do you manage campaigns on?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Twitter/X. We recommend platforms based on where your target audience actually spends time.

How do you measure influencer ROI?

Every campaign includes UTM-tagged links, conversion pixels, and promo codes. We track the full funnel from impression to sale and report actual revenue generated.
