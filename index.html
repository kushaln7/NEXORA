COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Clear answers about how we work, project timelines, pricing, and post-launch support.

What makes your development process different? +
We work in short, transparent milestones with regular demos and updates, so you’re never left wondering about progress. You’ll always know what’s being built, what’s coming next, and have opportunities to provide feedback throughout the project. No account managers or communication silos — you talk directly with the engineers and designers building your software.
How quickly can we start? +
Most engagements begin within two to four days. A focused Shape sprint is often the fastest way to align scope, team, and technical direction before a larger build.
Do you work with existing product teams? +
Yes. We frequently embed with internal teams, take ownership of a critical workstream, or provide a complete senior squad. The operating model is shaped around your capability gaps.
How much does a typical project cost? +
Every project is different, so we don’t use generic fixed packages. After a discovery discussion, we provide a detailed proposal with clear scope, timeline, milestones, and transparent pricing — so you know exactly what you’re paying for. No hidden costs or surprise invoices.
Can you support the product after launch? +
Yes. We offer focused optimization periods and longer-term product pods for teams that want continuity through user adoption, infrastructure telemetry, and product scale.
