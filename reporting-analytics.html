Frequently Asked Questions

What analytics tools do you work with?

GA4, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Tableau, BigQuery, Segment, and custom data pipelines. We recommend tools based on your data volume and team capabilities.

Can you fix our existing GA4 setup?

Yes. Most GA4 implementations we audit have significant tracking gaps. We fix event configurations, set up proper conversion tracking, and implement server-side tracking where needed.

How long does a full analytics setup take?

Typical implementations take 2-4 weeks — from data audit through dashboard delivery. Complex multi-platform integrations may take 4-6 weeks.
