Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build on Shopify or custom?

We build fully custom e-commerce platforms for maximum performance and flexibility. For teams that need Shopify, we build custom Shopify themes and apps — never off-the-shelf templates.

Can you integrate with my existing inventory system?

Yes. We build custom API integrations with ERP systems, warehouse management tools, and fulfillment providers for real-time inventory and order sync.

How do you handle payment security?

We use PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Stripe, Razorpay) — your platform never stores raw credit card data. All transactions are encrypted end-to-end.
