Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a WordPress site?

We build custom — no themes, no plugins, no bloat. Your site loads in under 1 second, scores 100/100 on PageSpeed, and can't be hacked through vulnerable plugin code.

Can my team update content themselves?

Yes. We integrate a headless CMS (Strapi, Sanity, or Contentful) so your marketing team can edit text, images, and pages without developer involvement.

What's included in the SEO setup?

Full technical SEO: meta tags, Schema.org JSON-LD, XML sitemap, robots.txt, Open Graph, canonical URLs, image optimization, and Core Web Vitals tuning.
